# Light Charter - 90 Segundos

## Mission

Turn earthquake preparation from information remembered into decisions rehearsed, beginning with one family in one room.

## User

A mobile-first family member in Mexico City who has participated in announced drills but has never practiced a stressful, branching home scenario.

## Three-year view

If the slice works, 90 Segundos becomes a Mexican scenario library for homes, schools, and small businesses. Certified civil-protection partners can author local scenes and compare behavior across repeated rehearsals without collecting unnecessary identity data. Insurers and public institutions may sponsor access, but never receive an individual risk score.

## Non-negotiables

1. The product rehearses behavior; it does not pretend to predict a disaster.
2. It measures only actions inside the simulation; it does not claim real-world readiness.
3. It never exploits fear, grief, or a dead person's likeness.
4. Simulated and unavailable capabilities are labeled in the interface.
5. It offers three meaningful choices, never an infinite scenario feed.
